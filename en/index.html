AI solutions

We build technology that connects diagnosis and improvement in one pipeline.

Real-time walking environment monitoring

Detects pedestrians and environmental elements in roadside CCTV video, and finds the spots where vulnerable pedestrians such as older adults and people with disabilities struggle.

Building disaster risk analysis

Combines each building's vulnerabilities, hourly population exposure and emergency access, and uses AI agents to find the best fire preparedness and response strategy.

AI+BIM design generation

Generates street and space design alternatives from diagnosis results and user experience, and evaluates them from the pedestrian's point of view.

Urban change analysis

Estimates changes in land cover and urban heat from satellite and spatial big data, for cities in Korea and abroad.

Product

Urban-FIT

A daily-life information service for foreign residents in Korea, especially international students. Describe your commute, budget and living needs the way you would say them, and Urban-FIT shows the neighborhoods and places that fit you and why. It also takes reports of everyday inconveniences and diagnoses them.

  • FindNeighborhoods and places that suit you, for living and visiting
  • InhabitDiagnosis of neighborhood inconveniences from a space profile of street photos and local information
  • ThriveReports from residents turned into improvements by universities and local governments

It is free for foreign residents. Their reports and ratings feed the neighborhood diagnosis, and contracted universities and local governments receive reports with directions for improvement.

Research

We turn technology from urban spatial research into tools that work in the field.

Smart buildings and smart cities for everyone, including the aged and the disabled.The goal of the URI3AN Lab at Ewha Womans University, which URBANARU shares.

Urban and building image analysis

Separates greenery, sidewalks, buildings and facilities in street and site photos. Trained on more than 5,000 street images, each rated for comfort by 50 or more people.

Place perception scoring

Estimates how comfortable and safe people find a place, and explains which elements drive the score.

Spatial data analysis

Combines accessibility, land use and population data to diagnose areas and find places that fit each person.

Our research has been published in international journals including Building and Environment and the Journal of Transport Geography.
